ABOUT POSTGEN

What is PostGen Web?

PostGen Web is a powerful LinkedIn content generation platform that combines artificial intelligence with professional writing expertise. Our dual-interface system provides both a local development environment (Studio) and a web-based interface for seamless content creation.

Key Features

🤖 AI-Powered Content Generation

Leverage multiple AI models including Ollama's Llama 3, GPT-4o, Gemini, and Puter AI to create engaging LinkedIn posts tailored to your professional voice.

🔗 LinkedIn Integration

Seamlessly fetch and analyze your existing LinkedIn content to maintain consistency in tone and style across all your posts.

🎨 Professional Themes

Choose from Light, Dark, and Blue themes to customize your workspace according to your preferences.

📊 Content Management

Edit, copy, and export your generated content in multiple formats including Markdown for easy sharing and archiving.

How It Works

  1. Connect: Link your LinkedIn credentials and API keys for enhanced functionality
  2. Generate: Use our AI models to create professional content based on your prompts
  3. Refine: Edit and customize the generated content to match your voice
  4. Export: Download or copy your content for immediate use on LinkedIn

Technology Stack

  • Frontend: Jekyll with GitHub Pages for seamless deployment
  • Backend: FastAPI integration with PostGen Studio
  • AI Integration: Ollama, OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Puter AI
  • Deployment: GitHub Pages with local development support

Getting Started

Ready to transform your LinkedIn content strategy? Start by:

  1. Setting up your LinkedIn credentials on the Home page
  2. Configuring your preferred AI model connections
  3. Exploring our latest updates and examples
  4. Creating your first AI-generated LinkedIn post

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This application is committed to protecting your personal data and ensuring transparency in how information is collected, used, and stored.

Data Collection and Usage

  • This application accesses LinkedIn data solely for the purpose of providing authorized services through the LinkedIn API.
  • All data retrieved via the LinkedIn API is processed locally and is not stored or retained without the user's explicit consent.
  • We do not collect or store sensitive personal information unless required for functionality and only with user permission.

API Credentials and Security

  • API keys, access tokens, and credentials are stored securely using industry-standard encryption methods.
  • These credentials are never shared with third parties and are used exclusively to authenticate authorized API requests.

Content Generation and External Services

  • Generated content is processed locally whenever possible.
  • External API calls are made only when explicitly configured by the user and are limited to services necessary for application functionality.
  • Any third-party service integrations will be clearly disclosed, and users will have control over enabling or disabling such features.

User Consent and Control

  • Users are informed about the data being accessed and have full control over granting or revoking permissions.
  • Consent is obtained prior to any data processing, and users may request deletion of their data at any time.

Compliance

  • This application complies with LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy and API Terms of Use.
  • We adhere to applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR and CCPA, where relevant.
